International Space Station

Or press Alt+Shift+S on x.com.

ISS works on X. Open a Space to get started.

  1. Open the panel on x.com and set things up.
  2. Start or join a Space and become a speaker.
  3. Play music, pads and reactions from the panel.

Wear headphones: ISS turns off echo cancellation so music sounds right.
